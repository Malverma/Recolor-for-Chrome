# Privacy Policy — Recolor for Chrome

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Recolor for Chrome does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any
personal or user data.

- **No data collection.** The extension does not read or record the pages you
  visit, what you type, your browsing history, or any account information.
- **No network requests.** The extension does not contact any server. There
  are no analytics, tracking, or ads.
- **Local storage only.** A wallpaper image you upload is saved only in your
  browser's local extension storage on your device. It never leaves your
  computer and is deleted when you remove the extension.
- **New Tab search.** Text typed into the New Tab search box goes directly to
  your browser's default search engine through Chrome, exactly as if you had
  typed it into the address bar. The extension does not see or keep it.

Questions: open an issue at
<https://github.com/Malverma/Recolor-for-Chrome/issues>.
